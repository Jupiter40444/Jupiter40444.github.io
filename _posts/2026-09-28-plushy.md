For this project I created a felt plushy that used conductive thread to light up three small light pins. The pins would flash when both a switch and button were toggled to "on". It was very important to connect 
positive and negative sides of the lights accordingly. For the decorative sewing component of the project I chose to add a bow. I stuffed the plushy with paper towel and used whip stitch to close it. I struggled a 
lot trying to get the lights to light up at first. I wish I had spent more time really focusing on the positive and negative side of the battery and prototyped with the alligator clips earlier on. When I trimmed the 
conductive thread to make sure there was absolutely no overlap that helped a lot as well. The main tip I would give to anyone trying to complete this project is to spend some time setting everything up with alligator
clips first.
