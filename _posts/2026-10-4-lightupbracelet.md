In this project I created a wristband. The snaps that closed the wrist band also completed a circuit that lights up an LED sewed into the band's felt. I started by creating a paper prototype, then tested it with alligator strips before sewing the electronic components into the felt. I then connected and sewed the pieces down using conductive thread, which I tested in conjunction with alligator clips along the way to make sure that everything was still working/would work in the end. The homemade button I used to complete the circuit in the middle of the band was a sun and moon made of conductive fabric. In keeping with that theme, I stitched a raincloud design on the bottom of the band using backstitch.
