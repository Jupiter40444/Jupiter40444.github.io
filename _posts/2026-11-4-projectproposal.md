Project Proposal: For my project I have three pieces, a t-shirt, a belt, and a felt necklace. I will sew a temperature sensor onto the t-shirt and if it detects a temperature greater than that of a burning building,
it will do a couple of things. It will buzz morse code for SOS on the vibe board sewn onto the t shirt, flash a red LED sewn onto a belt, and play "We Didn't Start the Fire" on the buzzer sewn onto the belt. However, it 
will only do these things if the two snaps sewn onto the t shirt connect to the two snaps sewn onto the belt. The necklace will be controlled independently of the Arduino, powered by a button in the same way the felt wristband was. 
If the switch attached to the necklace is switched on, it will turn on a couple LED's sewn onto the necklace.
