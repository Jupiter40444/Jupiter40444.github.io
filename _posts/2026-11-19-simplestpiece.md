The felt necklace: 
  The necklace has three red LED's that run on based on the status of a switch. The switch, LED's, and battery holder are connected via conductive thread which I've started 
  covering up with decorative red embroidery. It has a long way to go, but the end goal is to have a red, orange, and yellow flame design across the necklace following this design: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YgLpOdeJXGg. 
  I will also sew an extra piece of thread on the wrong side to prevent any overlapping of the conductive traces. The idea behind the necklace is to emit some light from the person wearing it in the event of the fire. 
Materials list: 
  Felt, conductive thread, embroidery floss, battery and battery holder, LED's. 
 2 tips to my past self:
  I would have been a little more careful with the conductive thread. In the end I had a little extra dangling off, and I needed to pin it down to make sure the connections went through. I also would have created a way to snap
  the necklace together, so it didn't have to be so long as to accommodate all neck sizes.
