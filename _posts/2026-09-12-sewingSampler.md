For my sewing sampler I chose the colors light blue and light pink. I decided to use split stitch and weaver wheel stitch for my personal stitch choices. I really liked the way the weaver
stitch looked and thought with the light pink color it looked like a rose. As for the split stitch it felt like it might be practically useful during the textiles part of this class. If I could
give a tip to myself before starting this project it would be not to be afraid or impatient with taking the fabric out of the embroidery hoop and to really take your time sketching out the stitches. 
It's kind of a pain to take the fabric in and out of the hoop but it's easier than having to redo the stitches which I definitely had to do a few times.
