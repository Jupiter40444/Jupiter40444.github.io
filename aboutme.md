### My Elevator Pitch

Have you heard of Meta Ray Bans? Google Glass? How about Ironman? Which one seems less dorky? My mission is to create wearable technology in the form of textiles, 
like a t shirt, pair of jeans, or a jacket. It could be anything from Spandex that plays disco music to a color changing t shirt. I'm still not quite sure yet. 
I hope you will follow along as I come up with the next great innovation in wearable technology.
